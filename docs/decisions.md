- Keyboard-first, local-first Firefox reader with plain JavaScript and no runtime build step.

- Planned Dropbox sync covers subscriptions, read states and settings, not bookmarks or article cache.
- Recommendations, sharing, tags, offline article downloads and separate bookmark sync are out of scope.
